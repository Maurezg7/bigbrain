## Condicionales

Una oración condicional tiene dos partes: la **condición** (con *if*) y el **resultado**.

| Tipo | Condición | Resultado | Uso |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cero | present simple | present simple | verdades generales |
| Primero | present simple | will + verbo | situación real o probable en el futuro |
| Segundo | past simple | would + verbo | situación imaginaria o improbable |
| Tercero | past perfect | would have + participio | pasado que no ocurrió |

- **Cero**: *If you heat water to 100 °C, it boils.*
- **Primero**: *If it rains, I will stay at home.*
- **Segundo**: *If I had more time, I would learn German.*
- **Tercero**: *If I had studied, I would have passed.*

> [!WARNING]
> Después de *if* **no va *will* ni *would***. Se dice *If it rains…*, no ~~If it will rain~~; *If I had…*, no ~~If I would have~~.

Detalles:

- En el segundo condicional se usa *were* para todas las personas en estilo cuidado: *If I **were** you, I would accept.*
- El orden puede invertirse; la coma se pone solo si la condición va primero: *I will stay at home if it rains.*
- En lugar de *will* y *would* pueden ir otros modales: *If you finish, you **can** leave. If I had money, I **could** travel.*

### Condicionales mixtos

Combinan tiempos distintos:

- Condición pasada, resultado presente: *If I **had taken** that job, I **would be** rich now.*
- Condición presente, resultado pasado: *If I **were** braver, I **would have spoken**.*

### Otras palabras de condición

| Palabra | Significado | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| unless | a menos que | I won't go unless you come. |
| as long as, provided (that) | siempre que | You can stay as long as you are quiet. |
| in case | por si | Take an umbrella in case it rains. |
| when | cuando (seguro) | When I arrive, I'll call you. |

## Wish e if only

Expresan deseos sobre algo que no es real. El verbo retrocede un tiempo, igual que en los condicionales.

| Deseo sobre | Estructura | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| el presente | wish + past simple | I wish I had a car. |
| el pasado | wish + past perfect | I wish I had studied more. |
| una conducta que molesta | wish + would | I wish you would listen. |

## Voz pasiva

Se usa cuando importa **la acción o quien la recibe**, no quien la hace. Es muy frecuente en textos científicos y técnicos.

Se forma con **be + participio pasado**. El objeto de la activa pasa a ser sujeto.

- Activa: *Fleming discovered penicillin.*
- Pasiva: *Penicillin **was discovered** by Fleming.*

| Tiempo | Activa | Pasiva |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | They make cars. | Cars are made. |
| Present continuous | They are making cars. | Cars are being made. |
| Past simple | They made cars. | Cars were made. |
| Past continuous | They were making cars. | Cars were being made. |
| Present perfect | They have made cars. | Cars have been made. |
| Past perfect | They had made cars. | Cars had been made. |
| Futuro | They will make cars. | Cars will be made. |
| Modal | They can make cars. | Cars can be made. |

El agente (*by…*) se omite cuando es obvio, desconocido o irrelevante: *The samples **were heated** to 80 °C.*

En inglés también el objeto **indirecto** puede ser sujeto de la pasiva, algo imposible en español:

- *They gave **me** a book.* → ***I** was given a book.*

Pasiva impersonal, típica del registro formal:

- *It **is said** that the method is reliable.*
- *The method **is said to be** reliable.*

### Have something done

Indica que otra persona hace algo por uno:

- *I **had** my car **repaired**.* (me arreglaron el auto)
- *She **is having** her hair **cut**.*

## Estilo indirecto

Al contar lo que alguien dijo, el verbo **retrocede un tiempo** si el verbo introductorio está en pasado.

| Directo | Indirecto |
|---|---|
| "I **am** tired." | She said she **was** tired. |
| "I **am working**." | He said he **was working**. |
| "I **saw** it." | She said she **had seen** it. |
| "I **have finished**." | He said he **had finished**. |
| "I **will** call." | She said she **would** call. |
| "I **can** help." | He said he **could** help. |

También cambian los pronombres y las referencias de tiempo y lugar: *today → that day*, *tomorrow → the next day*, *yesterday → the day before*, *here → there*, *this → that*.

Si lo dicho sigue siendo verdad, el cambio de tiempo es opcional: *He said the Earth **is** round.*

### Say y tell

- ***say** something*: *She **said** (that) she was tired.*
- ***tell** someone something*: *She **told me** (that) she was tired.*

### Preguntas indirectas

Pierden el orden de pregunta y el auxiliar *do*:

| Directa | Indirecta |
|---|---|
| "Where **do you** live?" | He asked me where **I lived**. |
| "**Are you** ready?" | She asked **if** I was ready. |

Lo mismo en preguntas corteses: *Could you tell me where **the station is**?* (no ~~where is the station~~).

### Órdenes y pedidos

*"Sit down."* → *He **told** me **to sit** down.* · *"Please don't go."* → *She **asked** me **not to go**.*

## Oraciones de relativo

| Pronombre | Se refiere a | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| who | personas | the student who won |
| which | cosas | the book which I bought |
| that | personas o cosas | the book that I bought |
| whose | posesión | the man whose car was stolen |
| where | lugar | the city where I was born |
| when | tiempo | the day when we met |

**Especificativas** (sin comas): identifican de quién o de qué se habla. Admiten *that*, y el pronombre se puede omitir si es objeto: *The book (that) I bought is good.*

**Explicativas** (entre comas): agregan información extra. No admiten *that* ni la omisión: *My brother, **who** lives in Salta, is an engineer.*

## Gerundio o infinitivo

Algunos verbos exigen una forma determinada del verbo que sigue.

| Seguidos de -ing | Seguidos de to + verbo |
|---|---|
| enjoy, avoid, finish, mind, suggest, keep, consider | want, decide, hope, need, plan, promise, agree, learn |

- *I enjoy **reading**.* · *I decided **to stay**.*
- Después de una **preposición** siempre va -ing: *I'm interested **in learning** German.*

Algunos cambian de significado:

| Verbo | + -ing | + to |
|---|---|---|
| stop | dejar de hacer: *I stopped smoking.* | parar para hacer: *I stopped to smoke.* |
| remember | recordar algo hecho: *I remember locking the door.* | acordarse de hacer: *Remember to lock the door.* |
| try | probar a ver qué pasa: *Try restarting it.* | esforzarse: *I tried to open it.* |
