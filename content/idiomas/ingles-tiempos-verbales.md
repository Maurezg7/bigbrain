## El sistema completo

El inglés combina tres **tiempos** (pasado, presente, futuro) con cuatro **aspectos**. Con el verbo *work*:

| | Pasado | Presente | Futuro |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | I worked | I work | I will work |
| **Continuo** | I was working | I am working | I will be working |
| **Perfecto** | I had worked | I have worked | I will have worked |
| **Perfecto continuo** | I had been working | I have been working | I will have been working |

## Present simple y present continuous

- **Present simple**: hábitos, hechos generales y horarios. *Water boils at 100 °C. She studies every day.*
- **Present continuous** (*am/is/are* + *-ing*): algo que ocurre ahora o de forma temporal. *She is studying for her exam right now.*

> [!WARNING]
> En tercera persona del singular el present simple lleva **-s**: *he works*, *she studies*, *it goes*. En preguntas y negaciones la -s pasa al auxiliar: *Does he work? He doesn't work.*

## Past simple y present perfect

Es la distinción que más cuesta, porque no coincide con el español.

| | Past simple | Present perfect |
|---|---|---|
| Forma | verbo en pasado (*-ed* o irregular) | *have/has* + participio |
| Uso | acción terminada en un momento **definido** del pasado | acción pasada **conectada con el presente**, sin decir cuándo |
| Marcadores | *yesterday, in 2020, last week, ago* | *ever, never, already, yet, since, for, just* |
| Ejemplo | *I finished the report yesterday.* | *I have finished the report.* (ya está listo) |

> [!NOTE]
> ¿*I lived in Salta for five years* o *I have lived in Salta for five years*?
>
> Las dos son correctas, pero significan cosas distintas. La primera dice que **ya no** vivís ahí. La segunda, que **todavía** vivís ahí.

## Futuro

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| *will* + verbo | decisiones espontáneas, predicciones, promesas | *I'll help you.* |
| *be going to* + verbo | planes ya decididos, predicciones con evidencia | *I'm going to study engineering.* |
| Present continuous | citas y arreglos ya fijados | *I'm meeting my tutor at five.* |
| Present simple | horarios | *The class starts at 8.* |

## Verbos irregulares esenciales

| Infinitivo | Pasado | Participio | Significado |
|---|---|---|---|
| be | was / were | been | ser, estar |
| have | had | had | tener |
| do | did | done | hacer |
| go | went | gone | ir |
| take | took | taken | tomar |
| write | wrote | written | escribir |
| know | knew | known | saber, conocer |
| give | gave | given | dar |

## Preguntas y negaciones

En los tiempos simples se usa el auxiliar ***do***; en los demás, el auxiliar del propio tiempo pasa adelante del sujeto.

- *Do you speak English?* — *I don't speak French.*
- *Did she call?* — *She didn't call.* (el verbo vuelve al infinitivo)
- *Are you listening?* — *Have you finished?* — *Will they come?*

> [!TIP]
> Para elegir el tiempo, hacete dos preguntas: **¿cuándo?** (pasado, presente, futuro) y **¿cómo lo veo?** (un hecho completo → simple; en desarrollo → continuo; con efecto en otro momento → perfecto).
